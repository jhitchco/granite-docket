Meeting calendars

Put any New Hampshire town's public meetings on your own calendar. Subscribe once and new agendas and hearings appear on their own as towns post them.

Each event links the agenda, notices and minutes filed for that meeting. Times come from the agenda when it states one; otherwise the event shows as all-day. Feeds cover 60 days back and 120 days ahead and refresh with each crawl. Always confirm with the town before you go: meetings move.
