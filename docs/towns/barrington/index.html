Barrington Conservation Commission agenda/minutes 5/28/26: Nottingham data center proposal

The Conservation Commission listed the Nottingham data center proposal under New Business. Residents raised concerns that the site (the former USA Springs warehouse) abuts Barrington conservation land and wetlands, and about noise, water and power demand. The commission offered support letters to Nottingham's Conservation Commission (minutes: https://www.barrington.nh.gov/conservation-commission/minutes/2026barrington-conservation-commission-meeting-minutes-0).
