Planning Board debating small reactors while studying data center rules

What they seem to be thinking

Over several months of data center hearings, board members and residents kept raising small modular reactors as the likely power source. The board appears split between a pro-nuclear view (clean, closed-loop) and safety, cooling-water and oversight worries. No nuclear-specific action yet; any rule would likely ride along with a data center ordinance and the Master Plan.

The good
  • Seen as clean power for data centers
  • Closed-loop cooling cited
  • Small footprint
The bad
  • Fire and safety fears
  • Cooling water disposal
  • Regulatory oversight questions
  • Imported technology doubts

What they seem to be thinking

Salem officials appear to see small modular reactors as a way to attract data centers and AI infrastructure and to be among the first NH towns to allow them. The draft district allows them subject to NRC licensing, later narrowed to land west of I-93. The council discussed them at length and a former planning director asked what they meant, so scrutiny is likely; final adoption is not shown in the record.

The good
  • data center and AI power supply
  • clean energy for businesses
  • first-mover appeal
  • NRC licensing as safeguard
The bad
  • proximity to residential areas
  • building height debate
  • public needs to understand the technology

Most telling: Town Council (EDC recommendation memo), 2025-08-04 — “Advanced Nuclear Technologies, including Small Modular Reactors (SMRs), microreactors”

Energy Commission report weighs a role for small reactors

What they seem to be thinking

The city's Energy Commission devoted a report section to whether small modular reactors fit NH, prompted by Governor and legislative interest. It frames nuclear economics against cheap natural gas and notes public opposition has stalled nuclear. No board action; likely to stay an advisory discussion unless state siting moves forward.

The good
  • state interest in SMRs
  • stable baseload power
  • municipal energy-cost management
The bad
  • competes with low-cost gas
  • NIMBY opposition
  • unproven economics

Most telling: Energy Commission, undated — “Is There a Role for Small Nuclear Reactors?”
